Refund & Cancellation Policy

SiloLink · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This page explains when orders can be cancelled and when refunds may apply. It works together with the Terms of Service.

1. Before payment

You may cancel freely. You are not charged. The listing can remain available to others.

2. Paid — goods not yet collected

If you have paid but the driver has not collected goods (or self-pickup has not completed), you may request a full refund. The order is cancelled and stock can return to the market. A driver who accepted but has not picked up is not paid for that job.

3. After pickup (driver has the goods)

Cancellation is not automatic. Open a dispute in the order. Outcomes may include full refund, partial refund, complete delivery, or return instructions after review. A fair logistics fee may apply if the driver already moved the goods.

4. After delivery is confirmed

No automatic refund. Report a problem within 24 hours with clear details and photos where possible. Admin review decides the outcome.

5. Seller cancels after payment, before pickup

Buyer should receive a full refund. Repeated seller cancellations may lead to restrictions.

6. Unpaid orders

If payment is not completed in time, the order expires. You are not charged for an unpaid expired order.

7. How refunds are paid

  • Wallet payments → refunded to your SiloLink wallet where possible
  • Paystack payments → refunded to the original method when Paystack allows, or wallet credit if stated for that case

8. Disputes

Use Report problem on the order. The other party may respond. SiloLink admin may decide using order status, handoff/PIN records and evidence. Fraud or abuse may lead to account action.

9. Contact

Use in-app support or the official SiloLink contact channel for refund questions.
