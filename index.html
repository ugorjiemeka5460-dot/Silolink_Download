Wallet, tokens & referrals

How money and rewards work — clear and practical.

Wallet (₦)

Top up your account, pay for market orders, receive seller proceeds, and withdraw when the app allows. Your balance is in naira.

Silo Tokens

Tokens track rewards (about 1 token ≈ ₦1 for offsets). Use them to reduce part of a market purchase where the offset limit allows. Earn from real activity such as qualifying transactions and promotions set in the app.

Referrals

Share your invite link. When someone you invite joins and completes a qualifying transaction, you can earn a fixed naira bonus. Bonuses are capped (for example the first 5 successful referrals).

Checkout

Choose wallet or Paystack. If wallet funds are low, top up your account or pay another way.

What tokens are for

Offset part of a buy price, follow reward history, and benefit from activity promotions — not a separate “betting” product.

History

Wallet history lists top-ups, purchases, sales, withdrawals and rewards in one place.
