Wallet, tokens & referrals

Rewards are built to encourage real use of the marketplace and network growth.

Wallet (₦) Top up like a betting wallet, pay for orders, receive seller proceeds, and withdraw when available.
Silo Tokens 1 token ≈ ₦1 for offsets. Earn from activity (for example small % on transactions, feed posts, and promotions as configured).
Referrals Invite people with your link. After a referred user transacts, earn a fixed naira bonus — capped (e.g. first 5 successful joiners).

Checkout choice

Pay with wallet or Paystack. If wallet balance is low, top up or switch method.

Token offset

Use tokens to reduce part of a market purchase (subject to the app’s offset limit).

Full history

Wallet history shows top-ups, buys, sells, withdrawals and rewards in one place.
