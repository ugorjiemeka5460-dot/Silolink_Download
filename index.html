Who is it for?

What you can do

Tap a card to expand.

Browse listings, place orders, and follow status from request to delivery or pickup.
Open jobs on the board. Logistics and other roles accept the jobs that match them.
Request storage, manage facilities, and keep produce tracked in one place.
Message partners, share status updates, and keep order talks in-app.
Get help navigating the app and practical tips. Live web search is planned for a later update.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Save the APK when your browser asks.
  3. Open the file. Allow install from this source if Android asks.
  4. Open SiloLink — sign in or browse without an account.

No special account is required just to download. Use any phone browser.
