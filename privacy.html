Privacy Policy

SiloLink · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Data we collect

  • Account data (name, email or phone, profile photo)
  • Role and verification information you submit
  • Listings, orders, storage and logistics activity
  • Payment references (card data is handled by Paystack, not stored fully by SiloLink)
  • Location when you use maps, delivery or facility features
  • Chat and media you choose to send
  • Basic device and log data for security and reliability

2. How we use data

To operate accounts, marketplace, orders, logistics, storage, wallet, support, safety (including handoff PINs), fraud prevention and service improvement.

3. Sharing

We share data with other users only as needed for a feature (for example buyer, seller and driver details on an order), with payment providers (Paystack), cloud infrastructure (such as Firebase and media hosting), and when required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Media and chat

Images and videos you upload may be stored on cloud media services. Profile and group photos are visible to relevant users.

5. Retention

We keep data while your account is active and as needed for orders, disputes, security and legal obligations.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion via support, subject to open transactions and applicable law. Account deletion may be requested in the app or by contacting support.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures. No system is completely secure.

8. Children

SiloLink is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your location).

9. International processing

Infrastructure providers may process data in other countries. We choose established providers and apply available safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes may be notified in the app or on this site. Continued use after notice means acceptance where allowed by law.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: use the support contact published in the SiloLink app or official channels.
